Hosting on GitHub Pages

This blog is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, GitHub processes technically necessary data, including your IP address, and according to GitHub stores it in log files for security reasons. I have no access to these logs.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR: I have a legitimate interest in delivering the blog securely and reliably. Data may be transferred to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, so the European Commission's adequacy decision applies (Art. 45 GDPR); in addition, GitHub uses the Commission's standard contractual clauses. Details: GitHub's privacy statement.

No cookies, local fonts, language choice

The blog sets no cookies. Fonts are served from the same server as the blog. No connection to Google Fonts or other font providers is made.

The blog shows itself in your browser's language: German if your browser is set to German, otherwise English. If you choose the language yourself via “DE / EN”, your browser remembers that choice locally (localStorage). This information never leaves your browser and is only used to show you the language you chose.

Visitor counting with GoatCounter

To see which posts are read, I count page views with GoatCounter. The service is operated by Martin Tournoij in Ireland; data is stored on servers of Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany and Finland. The counting script is served from the same server as the blog; the count itself goes to sudo-make-it-simple.goatcounter.com.

GoatCounter sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It records the page you open, the page you came from, browser and operating system (without the full user agent), screen width, and the country derived from your IP address. The IP address itself is not stored. To recognise repeat visits, GoatCounter keeps an association in memory for up to eight hours. No data is shared with third parties.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR: I have a legitimate interest in knowing how many people read the blog without tracking individuals. Details: GoatCounter's privacy policy.

Links to other sites

The blog links to LinkedIn, XING, GitHub and Carrd, among others. Data only goes to these providers when you click a link. From then on, their privacy rules apply.

Contact by email

If you email me, I process your details to answer your message (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). I delete them once they are no longer needed for that purpose and no retention obligation applies.

The address is an Apple email service (iCloud). Emails therefore pass through servers of Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland. Details: Apple's privacy policy.
